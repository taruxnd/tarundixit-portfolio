# Copilot section visuals

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. The tool does not expose a model selector; a specific “Images 2.5” model was not selected.

All seven selected images are saved here as PNGs and displayed through Next Image. They are original editorial illustrations, not screenshots, genuine handwriting samples, press cuttings or evidence of the business outcomes. Product flow uses the existing product screenshots and platform preview.

The frames remain inline within the existing 640px reading column. Desktop frames use containment on white; phone frames retain the natural 3:2 aspect ratio to preserve handwriting size. Images load lazily and have descriptive alternative text. No image is cropped.
