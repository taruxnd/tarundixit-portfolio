## Plan and completed edits

2. Separate decisions from the walkthrough. Explain the planner-to-Copilot change and six-week scope once. Reserve Product flow for what users do.
3. Simplify the language. Use short sentences, concrete actions and concise captions. Retain Symptoms, Root cause, Hypothesis, User need, user control and progressive disclosure; keep plain-language explanations for the technical terms.
4. Reduce visual repetition. Keep five product screenshots and the platform comparison. Remove the extra spreadsheet, evolution, detailed moments and ideas screens from this page, plus the unrelated meme section. Assets remain available in the project.
5. Audit evidence. Remove the interview-count and team-count placeholders. Remove the four metrics explicitly marked as illustrative in the source. Preserve the funding and client outcomes supplied in the existing case study; no external verification was performed.
6. Proofread the rendered article. Check heading order, duplicate wording, captions, terminology and the distinction between a hypothesis and a measured result.
