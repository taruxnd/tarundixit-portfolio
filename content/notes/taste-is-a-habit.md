People talk about taste like it's something you're born with. I don't think it is. I think taste is mostly a habit of paying attention, repeated until it becomes instinct.

## Noticing, on purpose

The designers I admire most are relentless noticers. They'll stop mid-sentence to point at how a door handle tells you to push, or how a menu groups things in a way that just *feels* right.

That isn't a talent. It's a reflex they built by doing it thousands of times.

## How I practise

1. **Keep a swipe file.** Every time something makes me pause, I save it with one line on *why*. The why matters more than the screenshot.
2. **Rebuild things I love.** Copying a great interaction, detail by detail, teaches me more than reading about it ever has.
3. **Say it out loud.** If I can't explain why something is good, I don't understand it yet.

Taste compounds. A year of noticing small things on purpose changes what you see when you look at your own work, and that's the whole point.
