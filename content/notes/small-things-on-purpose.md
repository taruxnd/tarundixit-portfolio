There's a bee on my homepage. You can tap it and it headbutts the screen. Nobody asked for that, and it's one of the things people mention most.

I keep coming back to this: the details that make something feel *made by a person* are rarely on the roadmap.

## Why they matter

Small, deliberate touches tell people that someone cared, and caring is contagious. When a product sweats the tiny things, people trust it with the big ones.

## Keeping them small

The trick is keeping them cheap and keeping them out of the way:

- They should never slow down the main task
- They should reward attention, not demand it
- They should still feel good the tenth time

Ship the important thing first. Then leave a little room for one small thing nobody asked for. It's usually the part people remember.
