Most design reviews I've sat in are about the first visit. The onboarding, the empty state, the hero. It makes sense: that's what demos well, and that's what everyone sees in the screenshot.

But nobody falls in love on the first visit. They fall in love on the fifth.

## What changes on the second visit

The second time someone opens your product, they're not curious anymore. They came back for a reason. They have a job in mind, and every pixel that doesn't help with that job is now in the way.

- The welcome tour they skipped is now clutter
- The clever empty state is gone, replaced by their messy real data
- The thing they did last time should be one tap away

## A small habit

Before I call a flow done, I use it twice in a row. Then I close it, come back the next day, and use it again with real intent. The first pass shows me what's confusing. The second shows me what's annoying. The third shows me what's missing.

Annoying is the one that quietly kills products, because nobody reports it. They just stop coming back.

Design for the person who already knows where everything is. They're the one who stays.
